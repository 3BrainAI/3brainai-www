public record.

DEMO-EU-AT-FIS-01 · v0.1 public-safe release. The two-page record uses dated public optical evidence and a synthetic construction review question.

View the dated image comparison

The record compares 20 August 2023 with 19 August 2025 and describes a bounded visible change in large roofed footprints. WATCH concerns evidence sufficiency and further documentary review; it is not a negative project rating.

Source identifiers, hashes, the working retrieval envelope and reason codes remain in the original record. The review question is synthetic; no actual drawdown request or lender decision is represented.

Documentary and engineering evidence remain necessary. Completion, quality, occupancy, legal title, value and payment eligibility are not established.

Read the signal in context

A colour is not
a bank decision.

MeaningWhat it tells the reviewer
PendingThe question or required response remains open.
Limited evidenceThe available source is insufficient or unsuitable for the stated question.
Scoped acceptanceA particular response has been accepted within its defined scope and exclusions.
Issued bank recordA separate institutional act under the bank’s authority and procedure.

A review can remain pending while evidence is limited. Accepting a scoped reply does not change the quality of the source. The public Fischamend record retains its original WATCH terminology; it is not relabelled as a T38 model acceptance.
