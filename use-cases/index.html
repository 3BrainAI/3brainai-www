Use Cases

Model situations where trusted evidence changes the workflow.

3BrainAI is useful when an organisation already has data, reports and controls, but the evidence is fragmented, hard to compare or difficult to defend later.

The examples below are illustrative. They show when a Trusted Data Plane, CRI Evidence Packs or governed Records can help teams route attention, document uncertainty and prepare better review material.

Physical-asset evidence workflows

CRI is designed for physical-asset risk workflows where existing controls remain in place, but teams need better evidence between formal review points.

Construction loan monitoring between formal controls

Situation
A bank finances a construction, industrial or infrastructure project. It already receives borrower updates, site-visit notes and technical advisor reports. The gap appears between formal controls.
What usually happens
Progress evidence sits across reports, emails, photos and provider formats. If the project later enters stress, the team must reconstruct what was known, when and why.
What 3BrainAI produces
A weekly or biweekly Evidence Pack for the monitored location, with OK / WATCH / INVESTIGATE, confidence, reason codes, explicit Data Quality Uncertainty and traceable input references.
Who reviews it
Project finance, credit risk, monitoring teams, workout teams, committee or audit reviewers.
Pilot format
2–3 monitored locations, 8–12 weeks, shadow mode only. Evidence Packs are reviewed in parallel with existing controls and do not affect live credit or risk decisions.
Business effect
The value is not replacing the site visit. The value is better timing: the team sees which project deserves attention before the next formal review point.

Portfolio triage when the team cannot review every site deeply

Situation
A lender, infrastructure owner or asset manager monitors 20–50 significant projects or locations. Reviewing every site with the same depth every cycle is slow and expensive.
What usually happens
Teams spend attention evenly until a visible issue appears. Stable, uncertain and risky cases compete for the same expert time.
What 3BrainAI produces
Comparable Evidence Packs across the portfolio, so stable cases remain visible while WATCH and INVESTIGATE cases receive deeper review.
Who reviews it
Portfolio risk owners, monitoring teams, infrastructure asset managers and internal reviewers.
Pilot format
Start with a small monitored sample, then expand to a portfolio if the output is readable, useful and routable inside the organisation.
Business effect
The value is review capacity. The organisation can route expert attention instead of treating every project equally.

Audit, dispute or later reconstruction

Situation
A project enters delay, cost stress, dispute, restructuring or internal review. The organisation needs to reconstruct the monitoring story.
What usually happens
Evidence is distributed across reports, images, inboxes, technical notes and versions. Later reconstruction is slow, stressful and hard to defend.
What 3BrainAI produces
Versioned Evidence Packs with timestamps, confidence, reason codes, uncertainty and publication history.
Who reviews it
Audit teams, risk committees, legal/compliance, workout teams and assurance reviewers.
Pilot format
A historical case can be used to test whether Evidence Packs would have made the review trail clearer.
Business effect
The value is reduced reconstruction pain. The organisation has a structured evidence timeline instead of a late forensic search through fragmented materials.

Public infrastructure oversight

Situation
A public body, city, infrastructure operator or funding authority needs a consistent view of multiple infrastructure projects or locations.
What usually happens
Reports arrive in different formats and at different levels of detail. Escalation depends on manual interpretation and fragmented evidence.
What 3BrainAI produces
Evidence Packs for selected assets or projects, designed for review meetings, escalation and documentation.
Who reviews it
Infrastructure owners, municipal or public-sector oversight teams, programme managers, audit or assurance reviewers.
Pilot format
One project, one asset class or one historical infrastructure case can be tested in shadow mode.
Business effect
More consistent oversight across projects, clearer escalation and better documentation for later review.

Insurance and assurance review

Situation
An insurer, assurance team or technical reviewer needs a traceable evidence timeline for a site, asset or claim-related situation.
What usually happens
External evidence is reviewed late or manually assembled from several sources.
What 3BrainAI produces
A review-ready evidence record that makes uncertainty visible instead of hiding it.
Who reviews it
Insurance risk teams, assurance reviewers, technical experts and audit teams.
Pilot format
A small set of monitored locations or historical claims can be used to test readability, routing and evidence value.
Business effect
Fewer unsupported assumptions, clearer escalation and a reusable evidence trail for expert review.

Governed records and institutional outputs

Records use the same governance discipline for catalog, registry, supplier, asset or institutional record workflows.

City or public registry consistency

A city or public institution works with records across departments, suppliers and documents. 3BrainAI can structure a Trusted Record Card with facts, constraints, reasons and review state.

Supplier and contractor qualification

A buyer needs to check whether a supplier, contractor or partner has the required documents, certificates, references or declarations. 3BrainAI can create a governed qualification record: what is available, what is missing, what expired and what needs review.

Catalog or asset record governance

A company manages product, asset or supplier records across systems. 3BrainAI adds provenance, controlled mappings, confidence and review state before records are published or reused.

When it makes sense

Use 3BrainAI when

  • evidence is fragmented across reports, files, systems or providers,
  • the workflow already has human review and accountability,
  • decisions may need to be defended later,
  • portfolio scale makes manual attention hard to route,
  • uncertainty must be visible rather than hidden,
  • an institution-specific PoC can be reviewed in shadow mode alongside the current process.

When it is not the right fit

  • the organisation wants automated final decisions,
  • there is no responsible reviewer or escalation process,
  • the problem is only raw data storage,
  • a one-off visual inspection is enough,
  • the available data is too weak and no review context exists,
  • the customer expects AI to replace expert judgement.
