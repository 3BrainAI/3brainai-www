A public record with an explicit boundary

The Fischamend illustration shows what a versioned Evidence Pack contains: a synthetic construction question, dated public observations, a bounded conclusion, uncertainty and the next documentary review step. It is a public example of the output, not a bank reference or a completed customer pilot.

CRI is the review-support product being developed. The Evidence Pack is its versioned review record: the output a reviewer can inspect, challenge and use within an agreed process.
