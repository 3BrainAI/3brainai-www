Security

High-level security posture for the public site and safe ways to reach the team about security topics.

Governance-first approach

3BrainAI follows a governance-first security and risk posture oriented around traceability, review-first outputs, controlled publishing and auditability. This framing applies to how product surfaces are designed to be operated, not to detailed implementation on this public page.

What this public website is not

The public website does not provide access to internal systems, customer environments, pilots, archives or data rooms. Do not rely on public email to exchange sensitive operational or contractual materials unless you already have an agreement that defines how those materials should be transferred and handled.

Security-related contact

For responsible disclosure or other security-related contact, email contact@3brain.ai with the subject line Security inquiry - 3BrainAI.

Product security principles

Across product surfaces, baseline expectations include least privilege access, traceable access patterns, a controlled release process, deliberate human review where it matters for risk, and an infrastructure posture oriented toward EU hosting paths where feasible. Detailed architecture, tooling choices and procedural documentation are intentionally not published here.
