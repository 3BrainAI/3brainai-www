Report a security concern

Email contact@3brain.ai with the subject Security inquiry - 3BrainAI.

In your first message, identify the affected public page or service and briefly describe the concern. Include a way to contact you. Do not include customer data, passwords, access tokens or exploit details; ask to agree an appropriate channel before sharing sensitive technical information.

This is an email contact route. Sending a message does not authorise access to systems or security testing.

Public information and controlled access

The public website contains product information and illustrative Evidence Packs. Its enquiry forms prepare email drafts locally; they do not upload files, provision accounts or grant access to internal systems or customer environments.

The Brief is a separate professional section with individually agreed access. A public link or an access enquiry does not bypass its sign-in or access requirements. Customer pilots and exchanges of sensitive material require a separately agreed scope and channel.

How the public website handles information

Accountable review

Construction Risk Intelligence (CRI) is designed to organise evidence and its limitations for professional review. The institution retains its own decision-making authority.

The current workflow is in controlled-case Proof of Concept validation. Public examples illustrate the review approach; they do not establish production deployment, certification or an independent security assessment.

Governance and due diligence

For product boundaries, accountable roles and scoped due-diligence enquiries, see Security, Governance & Assurance.

Detailed architecture and sensitive operational documents are discussed individually, with purpose, scope and confidentiality terms agreed before disclosure. An enquiry does not automatically provide access to documents or systems.
