Satellite observations and project records

How satellite evidence supports a bank review

3BrainAI is developing Construction Risk Intelligence (CRI) to bring dated satellite observations together with authorised project records in a versioned Evidence Pack. For a construction lender or collateral reviewer, the useful question is what the available evidence supports, what remains uncertain and which document or expert response is needed next.

Satellite imagery can provide dated context about visible changes at a site. It cannot, by itself, establish engineering completion, contractual acceptance or eligibility for a loan drawdown. CRI is currently in controlled-case Proof of Concept validation; institution-specific use requires an agreed scope and human review.

The four distinct meanings in a record

The review grammar

From context to a responsible review state.

The four meanings remain distinct throughout the record.

  1. 01 · Declared

    Review context

    What the hypothetical or institution-provided question says should be tested.

  2. 02 · Observed

    Dated evidence

    What the controlled source record supports within a stated observation boundary.

  3. 03 · Governed

    Uncertainty and reasons

    What remains partial, unverified or outside the permitted inference.

  4. 04 · Output

    Human-review state

    A reviewable record, not an autonomous credit or payment decision.

Two bank questions. One evidence discipline.

Use the record where the question arises.

Construction finance

Examine a milestone declaration against the relevant baseline and technical records. Keep the scope of a previous acceptance distinct from the work now presented for review.

Collateral & portfolio review

Focus follow-up between formal inspections. Ask whether a dated change is relevant to the monitored asset or one of its dependencies, and whether the next step needs documents, an explanation or a site visit.

Imagery supplies dated, surface-visible context. It does not establish property value, legal title, occupancy, hidden condition or protection against theft.

  2. Target workflow Candidate evidence state Bounded findings, reason codes and uncertainty.

Evidence suitability

A gap remains a gap.

Optical & aerial evidence

Useful surface context depends on resolution, coverage, season and the actual review question. An aerial image alone cannot confirm a milestone.

Radar & documents

Radar may add a different observation capability where it is suitable. Technical and project records remain necessary for questions an image cannot answer.

No data / no change

Insufficient observation cannot establish that nothing changed. The pack keeps the gap and the required follow-up visible.
