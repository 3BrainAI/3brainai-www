Review-first boundary

Governed evidence, not raw imagery.

CRI turns project context and governed input media into Evidence Packs for human review. It does not replace inspections, advisors or institutional decisions.

Human review Traceable inputs Explicit uncertainty Audit trail

The gap is not image availability. The gap is review-ready evidence.

Banks and institutional owners already have reports, site visits, technical advisors and internal controls. The difficult part is comparable, time-stamped and traceable evidence between formal review points.

How it works

Earth Observation is one governed input source. Project context and operational data can also be used. CRI converts inputs into constrained evidence states and review-ready outputs.
