The review grammar

From declared context to a responsible human-review state.

CRI is designed to keep four meanings separate so that a dated observation does not become an unsupported institutional conclusion.

  1. 01 · Declared

    Review context

    What the synthetic or institution-provided question says should be tested.

  2. 02 · Observed

    Dated evidence

    What the controlled source record supports within a stated observation boundary.

  3. 03 · Governed

    Uncertainty and reasons

    What remains partial, unverified or outside the permitted inference.

  4. 04 · Output

    Human-review state

    A reviewable record, not an autonomous credit or payment decision.

How it is designed to work

From controlled inputs to a versioned Evidence Pack.

Earth Observation is one intended input source. CRI is designed to convert project and observation inputs into constrained candidate evidence states and review-ready outputs.

Target workflow

  1. Target workflow Project + observation inputs Controlled context, reports and observation media.
  2. Target workflow Candidate evidence state Bounded findings, reason codes and uncertainty.
  3. Target workflow Human review Confirm, refine or reject the candidate state.
  4. Prototype format Versioned Evidence Pack Controlled review record with visible provenance and review state.

Validation and adoption path

The current Proof of Concept tests the governed workflow on controlled cases. An institution enters through an Evidence Readiness Check and may then agree a bounded validation in shadow mode. If its agreed criteria are met, the next step is a contracted paid pilot.
