Public information website

This website is a public information website. It is intended to explain what 3BrainAI does at a high level and to provide stable contact entry points. It does not offer authenticated accounts or an application workspace on the public site.

Contact and email

Contact is currently handled through mailto links, not a backend form on this site. If you send an email, the information you include is used only to understand and respond to your inquiry, and for ordinary communication admin (for example routing and thread handling).

Do not send confidential or regulated information through public email unless you have a prior agreement that defines how such information may be exchanged.

For Records / record-layer inquiries, CRI, investor introductions, partnerships or general questions, prefer the routed contact channels listed on Contact.

Fonts

This site may load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts as part of the current stylesheet setup. If you block third-party font requests, the site should still remain readable using system font fallbacks.

Updates
