Trusted Data Plane

The reusable governed operating layer behind CRI.

The Trusted Data Plane is the intended reusable operating layer behind CRI. Direction: a governed, AI-ready data backbone for regulated decisions.

Current public evidence is limited to illustrative Evidence Pack prototypes. The reusable layer and broader backbone remain target architecture and direction.

Target workflow

Inputs → Controlled records → Evidence state → Human review → Versioned output

  1. InputsProject context, reports, systems and observation media.
  2. Controlled recordsSource references, rules, ownership and version handling.
  3. Evidence stateCandidate findings, reason codes and explicit uncertainty.
  4. Human reviewConfirm, refine or reject with a visible review state.
  5. Versioned outputControlled publication and version handling for a review-ready record.

Target reusable capabilities

Capabilities the governed layer is designed to provide

Provenance and source references

Designed to keep source, timestamps and input conditions visible through the workflow.

Data contracts and controlled mappings

Designed to make rules, mappings and constraints explicit before candidate outputs are reviewed.

Confidence and uncertainty states

Designed to represent uncertainty as a visible workflow state.

Reason codes

Designed to connect candidate evidence states to reviewable causes.

Human review gates

Designed to keep confirmation, refinement and rejection under human control.

Version and review state

Designed to keep source, version, uncertainty and review state together in the record.
