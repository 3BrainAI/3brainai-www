Trusted Data Plane

A governed data plane for review-ready evidence.

The Trusted Data Plane is the reusable operating layer behind 3BrainAI. It structures truth, exposes uncertainty and turns fragmented inputs into trusted records, Evidence Packs and safe action workflows.

The product is not a model. The product is the governed path from uncertain inputs to accountable outputs.

Inputs → Records → Evidence → Review → Trusted Output

InputsFiles, reports, systems, Earth Observation signals, project context and catalog data.
Record LayerCanonical records, provenance, data contracts, ownership and versioning.
Evidence EngineReason codes, confidence, uncertainty handling and consistency checks.
Review WorkflowHuman review, escalation, approval state, safe publish and rollback.
Trusted OutputEvidence Pack, trusted record, audit trail, structured export or controlled publication.

Core capabilities

Provenance and source references

Source chain, timestamps and input conditions stay visible through the workflow.

Data contracts and controlled mappings

Rules, mappings and constraints are explicit before outputs are produced.

Confidence and uncertainty states

Uncertainty is a workflow state, not a hidden model artefact.

Reason codes

Structured explanations connect evidence states to reviewable causes.

Human review gates

Escalation, approval and responsible handoff stay in human control.

Versioning, diff, rollback and audit trail

Outputs remain reconstructable for audit, assurance and later review.
