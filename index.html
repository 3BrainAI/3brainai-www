Business model: recurring trusted-output workflows.

Customers pay for governed, review-ready outputs connected to assets, projects, record sets or workflow scopes – not for raw imagery, generic AI usage or one-off reports.

monitored asset / governed record set × service level × reporting frequency
+ setup / calibration
+ escalation

For CRI, the paid unit is a monitored asset, project or portfolio location. For Records, the paid unit is a governed record set or workflow scope. The commercial path starts with shadow-mode validation, then moves to paid pilots and recurring workflow use.

Validation starts where requirements are strict.

We validate first in demanding European environments, with a strong focus on DACH-facing requirements and selected CEE use cases. After workflow validation, the same operating pattern can expand across Europe and, depending on the domain, to selected non-European markets.
