Pilots & Partners

Validate trusted evidence workflows in shadow mode.

We work with banks, infrastructure teams, public-sector stakeholders, insurers, corporates and technology partners to test review-ready evidence workflows before any live operational use.

Shadow-mode validation means that Evidence Packs or trusted records are used for review and learning only. They do not replace existing controls and do not affect live financial, operational or risk decisions.

How a pilot works

Select the caseA project, asset, portfolio location, record workflow or historical case.
Calibrate the contextMilestones, risk windows, cadence, data availability and review expectations.
Run shadow modeEvidence Packs or trusted records are generated for review and learning only.
Review togetherThe partner assesses readability, usefulness, uncertainty handling and escalation logic.
Decide next stepRefine the workflow, extend the pilot, prepare a paid pilot or define rollout conditions.

Suitable pilot areas

Physical-asset risk monitoring

Construction, infrastructure, energy, industrial and logistics assets, collateral locations.

Public and institutional records

City, state or institutional record workflows where consistency and auditability matter.

Enterprise record workflows

Catalog, supplier, asset, compliance or reporting records across teams and systems.

Verification and trust extensions

Machine-verifiable records, credentialed review states and audit-trail integrations.

Geographic validation path

We validate first in demanding European environments, with a strong focus on DACH-facing requirements and selected CEE use cases. After workflow validation, the approach can expand across Europe and selectively beyond Europe depending on the domain.

Public ecosystem signals include OVHcloud Startup Program and EY Startup Academy Frankfurt. Additional validation and partner discussions remain NDA-bound until approved for public disclosure.
