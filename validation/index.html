Before the conversation

A few practical answers.

Who can use the bank demonstrator?

Banking professionals. An external technical adviser may participate within the bank’s process; the bank determines the roles and authority.

What if imagery cannot answer the question?

We make the limitation explicit, consider suitable documentary or other evidence, refine the scope or stop. Missing observation is not evidence of no change.

Does requesting The Brief create access?

The Brief offers paid access and selected free evaluation invitations. Requests are reviewed for relevance; scope, permitted use, duration and any applicable fee are agreed before an invitation. There is no automatic access or online payment.

Are notes from The Brief transferred?

No. Add the question you want to discuss yourself. Do not include confidential client information in the initial enquiry.
