Readiness conditions

Three conditions for an Evidence Readiness Check

A suitable review question

A concrete question that arises between formal control points and can be tested without changing a live decision process.

Available project context and evidence

Enough historical or current context to state what was declared, what can be observed and where uncertainty remains.

A responsible human reviewer

A named role that can judge whether the Evidence Pack is readable, useful and appropriately bounded.

Questions

Boundaries before a PoC begins.

What does a PoC evaluate?

A PoC tests one bounded review question with controlled project context and responsible reviewers. It evaluates evidence readability, review usefulness, uncertainty handling and governance fit – not production performance or portfolio economics.

What is AI intended to do?

CRI is being designed around an Evidence Engine that will combine task-specific AI components with deterministic processing. The target workflow uses AI to support input preprocessing and turn observation and project data into candidate structured findings, reason codes and uncertainty states. Explicit rules and human review remain mandatory before a governed Evidence Pack can be published.

What data or integration is required?

The Evidence Readiness Check determines whether the review question has usable context and sufficient data. A PoC may use real, historical or otherwise controlled cases; data handling and any integration requirement are agreed for the specific institution and scope.

Does CRI replace professional assessment or bank policy?

No. CRI is designed to fit alongside existing controls and to keep decision ownership with the institution. It is not intended to make a credit decision, confirm overall engineering completion or authorise an institutional action.

What happens when the evidence is insufficient?

Insufficient evidence and interpretation limits should remain visible as uncertainty and evidence-sufficiency states. The responsible reviewer can refine the question, request additional context or stop the workflow.

How does CRI relate to the EU AI Act and banking governance?

CRI is being designed as a review-support workflow, not as an automated credit-decision system. The Evidence Pack format is designed to keep source, observation date, version, uncertainty and review state attached to the record. These principles are consistent with the broader direction of EU AI governance, including the emphasis placed, for relevant high-risk systems, on documentation, traceability and human oversight. The institution remains responsible for its decisions and for the legal classification of any deployed use.
