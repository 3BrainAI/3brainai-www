Bank validation

Validate the evidence workflow in shadow mode.

A low-risk path for banks and institutional lenders to test whether a governed Evidence Pack improves review between formal control points.

Shadow-mode outputs are used for review and learning only. They do not affect live financial, credit or risk decisions, and existing controls remain in place.

Readiness conditions

Three conditions for a useful first case

A suitable review question

A concrete question that arises between formal control points and can be assessed without changing a live decision process.

Available project context and evidence

Enough historical or current context to state what was declared, what can be observed and where uncertainty remains.

A responsible human reviewer

A named role that can judge whether the Evidence Pack is readable, useful and appropriately bounded.

Low-risk start

Begin with a focused discussion.

An initial discussion can use a real or historical review question without system integration, without affecting live decisions and without a purchase commitment.

The bank provides

  • the review question and decision context,
  • available project context and evidence,
  • known constraints and uncertainty,
  • a responsible human reviewer.

Review output

What the bank receives

A concise Evidence Pack

A versioned comparison of declared context and available observation, with status, provenance and explicit uncertainty.

A clear boundary explanation

What the output supports, what it does not establish and where mandatory human review remains.

A next-step recommendation

Continue, refine or stop. A paid pilot is considered only after agreed validation criteria are met.

Decision gate

Success means workflow fit, not automated decisioning.

The review asks whether the Evidence Pack is understandable, useful, governable and appropriately constrained alongside existing bank policy, site visits, technical advisers and internal controls.

Next step

Test the workflow before operational use.

Start with one suitable review question and a responsible human reviewer.
