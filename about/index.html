About

Founder-led, governance-first.

3BrainAI Nexus develops CRI: governed Evidence Packs for accountable human review of physical-asset risk.

3BrainAI is building CRI for European institutional markets, with an initial focus on DACH, Benelux and Central Europe. Prague provides a well-connected operating base across these regions.

Founder

Dušan Přikryl – Founder & CEO

LinkedIn profile

Dušan Přikryl founded 3BrainAI after working on both the physical and digital sides of accountability. From 2003 to 2009, under direct owner and shareholder mandates, he led crisis and CAPEX transformation on complex industrial and energy projects. He later built a second professional layer across e-commerce, product development, data operations, analytics and AI. CRI brings those two layers together: first-hand understanding of physical-asset risk and the product discipline required to turn fragmented inputs into governed, review-ready evidence.
