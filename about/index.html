About

Founder-led, governance-first.

3BrainAI is built around a practical belief: complex business data only creates value when it can be trusted, reviewed and used with control.

Founder perspective

3BrainAI is shaped by practical experience in e-commerce, construction, risk-governed execution and data operations. The same structural problem appeared across construction, CAPEX, e-commerce and data operations: critical decisions are still made on fragmented, uncontrolled data.

Clear entity and IP boundaries

3BrainAI operates through Czech legal entities with clear product and IP boundaries.

3BrainAI Nexus s.r.o. develops CRI and regulated evidence workflows for physical-asset risk monitoring.

3BrainAI Solutions s.r.o. holds selected governance-first data background and catalog / record-layer context that informs the broader Trusted Data Plane discipline.

Operating model

We work as a small accountable core team with senior technical capability and documented review discipline. Human review stays in the loop where it matters for risk. Outputs are traceable, review-first and safe to use.

European market perspective

3BrainAI is designed for European B2B and institutional environments where governance, accountability and data control matter. Validation starts in demanding European settings and can expand after workflow acceptance.
