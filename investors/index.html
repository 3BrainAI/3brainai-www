Investor overview

A bank-first wedge for governed physical-asset evidence.

3BrainAI Nexus is developing CRI: governed Evidence Packs for banks and institutional lenders reviewing construction, infrastructure and significant real-estate collateral between formal control points.

The product is not raw imagery and not an autonomous credit engine. It is a review-ready evidence workflow with explicit uncertainty, provenance, versioning and mandatory human review.

Bank problem

Formal controls leave evidence gaps between review points.

Banks already use site visits, technical advisers, internal controls and project reporting. The gap is a consistent way to structure what was declared, what the available observation supports and where uncertainty requires attention.

Fragmented inputs

Imagery, milestones, reports and expert notes often arrive without one comparable review structure.

Delayed attention

Material changes can arise between formal control points without a governed evidence signal.

Accountable decisions

Review teams need provenance, uncertainty and review state—not a black-box conclusion.

Product

The Evidence Pack is the customer-facing artefact.

CRI turns Earth Observation and project context into a versioned review pack with status, confidence, reason codes, uncertainty, provenance and mandatory human review.

Product boundary

  • Earth Observation is an input, not the customer-facing product.
  • A WATCH state routes evidence to a reviewer; it does not trigger a credit decision.
  • CRI complements bank policy and professional assessment.
