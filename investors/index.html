Bank problem

Add a governed evidence state between formal review points.

Banks already use site visits, technical advisers, internal controls and project reporting. CRI is designed to add a consistent record of what was declared, what the available observation supports and where uncertainty requires attention.

Fragmented inputs

Imagery, milestones, reports and expert notes often arrive without one comparable review structure.

Review routing

A governed evidence state is designed to help reviewers identify the next question between formal control points.

Accountable review

Review teams need provenance, uncertainty and review state – not a black-box conclusion.

Product

The 3BrainAI CRI Evidence Pack is the customer-facing artefact.

An Evidence Pack is a short, versioned review record that keeps status, reasons, uncertainty, provenance and review state attached to the evidence.

Product boundary

  • Earth Observation is an input, not the customer-facing product.
  • A WATCH state routes evidence to a reviewer; it does not trigger a credit decision.
  • CRI is designed to complement bank policy and professional assessment.
